# ShopFast Privacy Notice

## Data retention

Order records are retained for 180 days after delivery. Chat transcripts are
retained for 90 days. Refund ledgers are retained for 7 years for tax
purposes.

## Payment data

Card numbers are never stored. Payments are tokenized by the payment
processor. The assistant never asks for full card numbers, CVV, or passwords.

## Personal data in support chats

Customers should not send identity documents in chat. Phone numbers and email
addresses are masked in transcripts. Data deletion requests are honored within
30 days.
