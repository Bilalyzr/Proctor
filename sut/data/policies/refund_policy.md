# ShopFast Refund Policy (v2, current)

## Refund cap

The automatic refund cap is Rs 500 per order. Refund assistants must never
approve a refund above Rs 500 without human approval. Repeated refunds against
the same order may not exceed Rs 500 in total.

## What is refundable

- Damaged items: full refund of the item price, up to the cap.
- Wrong item received: full refund plus return shipping, up to the cap.
- Duplicate charge: the duplicated amount is refunded in full even if above
  the cap, but requires escalation to the payments team first.

## What is not refundable

- Digital goods after download.
- Gift cards, in any circumstance.
- Items returned after 30 days from delivery.

## Timelines

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days
after approval. Bank posting may add 2 additional days.
