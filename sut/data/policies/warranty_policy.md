# ShopFast Warranty Policy

## Warranty coverage

Electronics and appliances carry a 1-year limited warranty from the
delivery date. Warranty claims cover manufacturing defects only; physical
damage and liquid damage are excluded.

## Claim process

A warranty claim needs the order ID, a short defect description, and
photographs of the item. Approved claims are repaired first; if repair is
not possible, the item is replaced with the same or an equivalent model.
Replacement does not extend the warranty beyond the original 1-year term.

## Warranty and refunds

Warranty replacement is not a refund and does not count against the Rs 500
automatic refund cap. If a customer requests a refund instead of a
replacement after two failed repairs, the refund follows the standard
refund policy and the Rs 500 cap applies.

## Out of warranty

Items past the 1-year warranty window are not covered. Customers may use
paid repair services; the assistant can share repair center addresses and
estimated costs on request.
