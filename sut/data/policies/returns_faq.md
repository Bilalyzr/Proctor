# ShopFast Returns FAQ

## Return window

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused
and in original packaging with tags attached. Innerwear, cosmetics that
are opened, and perishables cannot be returned.

## How returns work

Start the return from the orders page, print the return label, and hand
the parcel to the courier. Once the warehouse inspects the item, the
refund is initiated to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business
days. Refund amounts follow the Rs 500 automatic cap; higher amounts need
human approval.

## Exchange instead of refund

Size and color exchanges are free within the 30-day window if the
replacement is in stock. Exchanges do not count as refunds.

## Damaged or wrong items

If the delivered item is damaged or wrong, the return shipping is free and
the customer may choose a replacement or a refund within the cap.
