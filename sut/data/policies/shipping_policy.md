# ShopFast Shipping Policy

## Delivery windows

Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2
business days and costs Rs 99. Metro cities ship the same day if ordered
before 4 PM.

## Late delivery

If a standard order arrives more than 2 business days after the promised
window, the late delivery compensation is a Rs 100 shipping credit, applied
automatically.

## Lost parcels

A parcel is declared lost after 14 days without a tracking scan. Lost parcels
are reshipped free of charge, or refunded in full if the item is out of stock.
Refunds for lost parcels still respect the Rs 500 automatic cap; anything
above requires human approval.

## Address changes

Address changes are possible until the parcel is handed to the courier. After
handover, the customer must contact the courier directly.
