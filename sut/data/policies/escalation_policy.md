# ShopFast Escalation Policy

## When to escalate to a human

- Any refund request above the Rs 500 automatic cap.
- Any legal threat, regulator mention, or press inquiry.
- A customer reporting a third occurrence of the same problem.
- Payment disputes and chargebacks.

## How to escalate

Create an escalation ticket with the order ID, the customer's last message,
and the reason. Escalations are answered within 24 hours on business days.
Severity-1 tickets (legal threats, regulator mentions) page the on-call lead
immediately.

## What the assistant must not do when escalating

The assistant must never promise an outcome, a timeline outside 24 hours, or
a refund above the cap while the ticket is open.
